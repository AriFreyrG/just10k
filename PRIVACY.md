# just10k privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

just10k plans walking loops. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store any of your data.

## What the app uses and why

**Location.** just10k uses your location to plan a loop that starts where you are and to guide you around it. To plan a loop, the app sends your start point and a few points around it to the free OpenStreetMap routing server at routing.openstreetmap.de, which returns a walking route. If you search for an address, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to look it up. Those services are run by third parties under their own privacy policies:

- FOSSGIS routing server: https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html
- OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim): https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

The app only asks for location while it is open. It does not track your location in the background.

**Motion and fitness (step count).** just10k reads your step count from your iPhone to work out how many steps you have left today and to count steps during a walk. This stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

**Settings.** Your step goal, height, stride and today's walked steps are saved on your phone only.

## Sharing

When you tap Share GPX or Open in Google Maps, the route is handed to the app or service you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Children

just10k is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
