## Description [4000]

Get your 10,000 steps without walking the same loop again.

just10k looks at how many steps you have left today and plans a walking loop from where you are, sized to match. It starts and ends at your door, so you always finish at home.

KNOWS WHAT'S LEFT
just10k reads today's step count from your iPhone and plans only the remainder. Set your own daily goal if 10,000 isn't yours.

A FRESH LOOP EVERY TIME
Get three loops to pick from, each sized to the distance you need. Swipe between them, or shuffle for new ones. Loops that double back on the same streets are ranked lower.

SHORT ON TIME?
Pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes instead, and just10k plans a loop that fits.

TURN BY TURN
Walk mode shows the next turn in big type, buzzes before each turn, and counts your steps as you go. The part you've walked fades out. Take a wrong turn and just10k can re-plan a new way home that still adds up to your goal.

YOUR LOOP, SAVED
When you finish, see the shape of today's loop with your steps, distance and time. Share it as a GPX file for Strava, Garmin and more, or open the route in Google Maps.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no tracking. Your step count stays on your phone.

Routes are planned with OpenStreetMap data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## App Privacy (draft — confirm before submitting)

- Data linked to you: none. No tracking.
- Location — Precise Location, used for App Functionality, not linked to identity, not used for tracking.
  Your start point and the route points are sent to the routing server
  (routing.openstreetmap.de) to plan a loop, and an address you type is sent
  to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find it.
- Health & Fitness (step count): read on device only, never sent anywhere,
  so it doesn't need declaring.
